This summer I'm joining the [Regeneron Genetics Center](https://www.regeneron.com/science/genetics-center) as a PhD intern with the Neuroscience & Ophthalmology Therapeutic Area Genetics team, working with [Ernst Mayerhofer](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Ojs5ZIgAAAAJ) and [Sahar Gelfman](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=r_HnSQsAAAAJ). The project connects GWAS hits to drug-response mechanisms in the [Tahoe-100M](https://arcinstitute.org/news/news/arc-vevo) perturbation atlas: how do germline variants found at population scale play out at the cellular level, and how do they interact with pharmacological intervention?
