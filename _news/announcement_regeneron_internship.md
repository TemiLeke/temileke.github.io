I'm happy to announnce that this summer I'll be joining the [Regeneron Genetics Center](https://www.regeneron.com/science/genetics-center) as a PhD intern with the Neuroscience and Ophthalmology Therapeutic Area Genetics team. I'll be working on connecting GWAS hits to drug response mechanisms in [Tahoe-100M](https://arcinstitute.org/news/news/arc-vevo) perturbation study. This project will address a fundamental question in precision medicine regarding how germline genetic variants identified through population-level GWAS studies manifest their effects at the cellular level, and how the effects interact with pharmacological interventions. Super excited to work on this with [Ernst Mayerhofer](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=Ojs5ZIgAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) and [Sahar Gelfman](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=r_HnSQsAAAAJ&hl=en).
